**Sales Trends Analysis (Google Sheets)**

An end-to-end sales analysis project built in Google Sheets, focused on identifying revenue, profit and sales trends across product categories and countries.

The project analyses historical sales data and presents business insights through an interactive dashboard and analytical report.


**Project Overview**

This project analyses 1,246 sales orders from 45 countries between 2010 and 2017. To better identify recent trends, the analysis focuses on 2016–2017.
The objective was to understand which products, countries and sales channels contributed the most to business performance and identify opportunities for improving inventory planning and sales strategy.


**Business Questions**

Which product categories generated the highest revenue and profit?
Which countries generated the highest revenue?
How does sales performance differ between online and offline channels?
Does delivery lead time affect profit?
Which categories dominate total revenue according to the Pareto principle?
Are there seasonal sales trends during the year?
Which weekdays generate the highest sales volume?


**Dataset**

Orders: 1,246
Countries: 45
Period: 2010–2017
Analysis period: 2016–2017


**The dashboard includes interactive visualisations for:**

Revenue by product category.
Profit by product category.
Revenue by country.
Online vs Offline sales.
Monthly sales trends.
Lead time analysis.
Pareto analysis (80/20).


**Key KPIs**

Total Revenue
Total Cost
Total Profit
Number of Orders
Number of Countries
Average Lead Time


**Key Insights**

Office Supplies generated the highest revenue.
Cosmetics produced the highest share of profit.
Household was one of the strongest-performing categories in 2017.
Revenue was concentrated in a small number of European countries.
Profit from online and offline sales was nearly evenly distributed.
Sales volume increased around weekends (Thursday–Monday).
Pareto analysis showed that approximately 80% of revenue came from six product categories.


**Business Recommendations**

Prioritise inventory planning for the six highest-performing categories.
Monitor delivery performance for Office Supplies due to longer lead times.
Focus marketing campaigns on high-profit categories such as Cosmetics and Household.
Analyse countries with the highest revenue separately to identify expansion opportunities.
